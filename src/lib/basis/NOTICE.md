# Basis Universal transcoder

`basis_transcoder.js` and `basis_transcoder.wasm` are the Basis Universal GPU
texture codec transcoder, used here to decode KHR_texture_basisu (KTX2) textures
to RGBA in the browser.

  (https://github.com/BinomialLLC/basis_universal).
- These prebuilt transcoder files are distributed with three.js
  (https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js, MIT) under `examples/jsm/libs/basis/`
  and are vendored unmodified.

No changes have been made to these files.
